- Reads `docs/product/definition.md`, not the implementation itself, to stay grounded in what was actually approved rather than guessing from the finished UI.
- Has an explicit guardrail: do not invent claims the product cannot back up. An SEO skill with no such instruction will happily oversell.

## Where it fits

Invoked by [Pre-launch activities](/handbooks/claude/showcase-pre-launch-activities), after [Validation & testing](/handbooks/claude/showcase-validation-testing) passes, alongside [Accessibility](/handbooks/claude/showcase-accessibility) and [UI/UX polish](/handbooks/claude/showcase-ui-ux-polish).
