- A sub-skill of [Implementation](/handbooks/claude/showcase-implementation), invoked first of its four.
- Reads `docs/product/planning.md` for the architecture. From here on, the sub-skills in this phase stop reading and writing `docs/product/` files, and start reading and writing the codebase itself.
- Writes migrations, not a hand-edited database, so the change is reviewable and repeatable, the same reason a hook runs a formatter instead of asking Claude to format by hand.
