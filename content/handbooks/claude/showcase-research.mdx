## Steps

1. Read docs/product/idea.md. If it does not exist, stop and invoke /understanding-of-idea first.
2. Trends: what is changing in this space right now, and why now is (or is not) a good time.
3. Competitors: direct and adjacent products solving the same or an adjacent problem, one line each, what they do well, and the gap this idea could fill.
4. Market: rough size and the specific first segment worth targeting, informed by the competitor list from step 3.

Write all three as one file, docs/product/research.md.`} />

## Why this stays one skill

An earlier version of this example split trends, competitor, and market research into three separate sub-skills, each its own file, each independently invocable. That added a fourth level to the tree for no real gain, none of the three is ever useful to run completely on its own mid-process, and market sizing needed competitor data anyway, which meant coordinating two separate skills instead of just reading the previous step's output. Numbered steps inside one file do the same job with one less layer to route through.
