<InOneMinute>

- A sub-skill of [Pre-launch activities](/handbooks/claude/showcase-pre-launch-activities), invoked alongside [SEO](/handbooks/claude/showcase-seo) and [Accessibility](/handbooks/claude/showcase-accessibility), once [Validation & testing](/handbooks/claude/showcase-validation-testing) passes.
- Covers what functional tests do not: a flow that works but feels clumsy, a missing loading state, an error message no one would understand.
- Draws the same line [Accessibility](/handbooks/claude/showcase-accessibility) does, fix the small, low-risk stuff directly, flag anything that would change layout or interaction significantly, rather than deciding alone.

</InOneMinute>

## Where it fits

Invoked by [Pre-launch activities](/handbooks/claude/showcase-pre-launch-activities), after [Validation & testing](/handbooks/claude/showcase-validation-testing) passes, alongside [SEO](/handbooks/claude/showcase-seo) and [Accessibility](/handbooks/claude/showcase-accessibility). None of the three depends on either of the others. Once all four sub-skills in this phase report back, the whole process is done.
