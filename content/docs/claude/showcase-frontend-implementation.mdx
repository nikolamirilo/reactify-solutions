<InOneMinute>

- A sub-skill of [Implementation](/docs/claude/showcase-implementation), invoked third of its four, after [Backend](/docs/claude/showcase-backend-implementation).
- Checks which endpoints the backend actually shipped, not just what the plan called for. Plans and code drift, and this is the sub-skill positioned to notice.
- Flags a planned feature with no working endpoint instead of building UI against an assumption that will fail in [Integration & deployment](/docs/claude/showcase-integration-and-deployment).

</InOneMinute>

## Where it fits

Invoked by [Implementation](/docs/claude/showcase-implementation), third of its four sub-skills, after [Backend](/docs/claude/showcase-backend-implementation). Hands off to [Integration & deployment](/docs/claude/showcase-integration-and-deployment), which runs all three built pieces together.
