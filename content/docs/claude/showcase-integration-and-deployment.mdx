<InOneMinute>

- A sub-skill of [Implementation](/docs/claude/showcase-implementation), invoked fourth and last of its four, after [Database](/docs/claude/showcase-database-implementation), [Backend](/docs/claude/showcase-backend-implementation), and [Frontend](/docs/claude/showcase-frontend-implementation) all exist.
- Its whole job is catching what only breaks when the three pieces run together, not re-testing each piece in isolation again.
- Reports a working, deployed build back to [Implementation](/docs/claude/showcase-implementation), which is what tells that phase it is safe to hand off to [Pre-launch activities](/docs/claude/showcase-pre-launch-activities).

</InOneMinute>
