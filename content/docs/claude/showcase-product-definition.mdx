## SKILL.md

<CodeBlock language="yaml" filename=".claude/skills/product-definition/SKILL.md" code={`---
name: product-definition
description: Turns idea and research into a defined product, scope, core features, and what is explicitly out. Use after research is complete, before planning. Not split into sub-skills, this phase stays one sequence of steps.
---
## Steps

1. Read docs/product/idea.md and docs/product/research.md.
2. Draft three candidate scopes: minimal, realistic, ambitious.
3. Ask the user to pick one, or push back and negotiate if none fit the research.
4. Write the chosen scope, core features, and an explicit "not building this yet" list to docs/product/definition.md.`} />

## Why this stays one skill

Step 2 cannot run before step 1 finishes, step 4 needs the user's answer from step 3, and nobody would ever invoke "draft three candidate scopes" on its own. When steps depend on each other in a strict line and none is independently reusable, numbered steps inside one `SKILL.md` are the whole answer. Splitting them into four sub-skills would only add files to route through, not flexibility. This is the shape every sub-skill in [Definition & planning](/docs/claude/showcase-definition-and-planning) ended up using, once the tree settled on staying three levels deep.

## Where it fits

Invoked by [Definition & planning](/docs/claude/showcase-definition-and-planning), third of its four sub-skills, after [Research](/docs/claude/showcase-research). Hands off to [Planning](/docs/claude/showcase-planning), which reads `docs/product/definition.md` as its own starting point.
