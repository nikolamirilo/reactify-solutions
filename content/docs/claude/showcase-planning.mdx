<InOneMinute>

- Phase 4 of 8, invoked after [Product definition](/docs/claude/showcase-product-definition).
- Splits into two sub-skills, business requirements and architecture, run in that order because architecture needs a budget and timeline before it can size a stack sensibly.
- Unlike [Research](/docs/claude/showcase-research)'s three independent sub-skills, these two have a strict dependency between them: business requirements always runs first.
- Waits for explicit user confirmation before handing off to implementation, since this is the last phase before anything gets built.

</InOneMinute>
