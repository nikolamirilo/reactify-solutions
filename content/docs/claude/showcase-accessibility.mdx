<InOneMinute>

- A sub-skill of [Pre-launch activities](/docs/claude/showcase-pre-launch-activities), invoked alongside [SEO](/docs/claude/showcase-seo) and [UI/UX polish](/docs/claude/showcase-ui-ux-polish), once [Validation & testing](/docs/claude/showcase-validation-testing) passes.
- Unlike most sub-skills in this example, it does not name a specific `docs/product/` file to read. It audits the running product directly against a named external standard, WCAG 2.2 AA.
- Draws a firm line between fixes it can make on its own, contrast, labeling, and ones that need a human call, a layout change, and refuses to blur that line. [UI/UX polish](/docs/claude/showcase-ui-ux-polish), its sibling, draws a similar line for usability rather than accessibility.

</InOneMinute>

## Where it fits

Invoked by [Pre-launch activities](/docs/claude/showcase-pre-launch-activities), after [Validation & testing](/docs/claude/showcase-validation-testing) passes, alongside [SEO](/docs/claude/showcase-seo) and [UI/UX polish](/docs/claude/showcase-ui-ux-polish). Once all four sub-skills in this phase report back, nothing in the tree runs after it. Start over from [the orchestrator skill](/docs/claude/showcase-orchestrator), or back out to the [Skills Showcase overview](/docs/claude/skills-showcase).
