<InOneMinute>

- Phase 2 of 3, invoked once [Definition & planning](/docs/claude/showcase-definition-and-planning) is confirmed.
- Invokes four sub-skills in a fixed, dependency-driven order: the database has to exist before the backend can query it, the backend before the frontend can call it.
- A deliberate contrast with phase 1: none of its sub-skills write to `docs/product/`. By this point the plan has become code, and the codebase itself is the handoff.

</InOneMinute>

## Its sub-skills

| Sub-skill | Depends on |
|---|---|
| [Database](/docs/claude/showcase-database-implementation) | `docs/product/planning.md` |
| [Backend](/docs/claude/showcase-backend-implementation) | The schema database-implementation just wrote |

## Where it fits

Invoked by [the orchestrator](/docs/claude/showcase-orchestrator) as phase 2, after [Definition & planning](/docs/claude/showcase-definition-and-planning). Hands off to [Pre-launch activities](/docs/claude/showcase-pre-launch-activities).
