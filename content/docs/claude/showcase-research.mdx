<InOneMinute>

- Phase 2 of 8, invoked by the orchestrator right after [Understanding the idea](/docs/claude/showcase-understanding-of-idea).
- It does none of the research itself. It invokes three sub-skills in a fixed order and merges what they produce.
- This is the first split in the tree: `research` is a phase skill *and* a parent to three narrower sub-skills.
- It checks its own precondition, `docs/product/idea.md` existing, before invoking anything, rather than assuming the orchestrator already handled that.

</InOneMinute>

## SKILL.md

<CodeBlock language="yaml" filename=".claude/skills/research/SKILL.md" code={`---
name: research
description: Coordinates trends, competitor, and market research for a new product idea. Use after understanding-of-idea is written, before product-definition. Invokes /trends-research, /competitors-research, and /market-research in turn.
---
## Read first

Read docs/product/idea.md. If it does not exist, stop and invoke /understanding-of-idea first.

## Invoke, in order

1. /trends-research
2. /competitors-research
3. /market-research

Each sub-skill writes its own file under docs/product/research/. Once all three exist, summarize them in docs/product/research-summary.md and hand off to /product-definition.`} />

- The description names all three sub-skills explicitly. That is what tells Claude "research" is not a leaf, and lets it route a request like "just do the competitor part" straight to [Competitor research](/docs/claude/showcase-competitors-research) without going through this file at all.
- "If it does not exist, stop" is a small but real safeguard. Without it, a user jumping straight to `/research` on a fresh project would produce research grounded in nothing.

## Its sub-skills

| Sub-skill | What it produces |
|---|---|
| [Trends research](/docs/claude/showcase-trends-research) | `docs/product/research/trends.md` |
| [Competitor research](/docs/claude/showcase-competitors-research) | `docs/product/research/competitors.md` |
| [Market research](/docs/claude/showcase-market-research) | `docs/product/research/market.md` |

## Where it fits

Invoked by [the orchestrator](/docs/claude/showcase-orchestrator) as phase 2, after [Understanding the idea](/docs/claude/showcase-understanding-of-idea). Hands off to [Product definition](/docs/claude/showcase-product-definition) once all three sub-skills above have written their files.
