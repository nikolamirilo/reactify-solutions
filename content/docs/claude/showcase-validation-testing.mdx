<InOneMinute>

- Phase 6 of 8, invoked after [Implementation](/docs/claude/showcase-implementation).
- Checks the product against two different, earlier files: the success definition from phase 1, and the scope from phase 3, not just whether the code runs.
- Its explicit job is to report scope drift, features that shipped but were not in the approved definition, not silently accept it.
- Runs before SEO and accessibility, on the reasoning that testing a product that does not do what it was supposed to do first is wasted effort on the other two.

</InOneMinute>
