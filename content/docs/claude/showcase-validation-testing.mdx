<InOneMinute>

- A sub-skill of [Pre-launch activities](/docs/claude/showcase-pre-launch-activities), invoked first of its four, right after [Implementation](/docs/claude/showcase-implementation) reports a working, deployed build.
- Checks the product against two different, earlier files: the success definition from `idea.md`, and the scope from `definition.md`, not just whether the code runs.
- Its explicit job is to report scope drift, features that shipped but were not in the approved definition, not silently accept it.
- The one sub-skill in this phase that gates the rest. [SEO](/docs/claude/showcase-seo), [Accessibility](/docs/claude/showcase-accessibility), and [UI/UX polish](/docs/claude/showcase-ui-ux-polish) all wait for this one to pass first.

</InOneMinute>

## Where it fits

Invoked by [Pre-launch activities](/docs/claude/showcase-pre-launch-activities), first of its four sub-skills, right after [Implementation](/docs/claude/showcase-implementation). Once it passes, [SEO](/docs/claude/showcase-seo), [Accessibility](/docs/claude/showcase-accessibility), and [UI/UX polish](/docs/claude/showcase-ui-ux-polish) can all run, in any order.
