<InOneMinute>

- A sub-skill of [Research](/docs/claude/showcase-research), invoked second of the three.
- Reads `docs/product/idea.md`. Writes `docs/product/research/competitors.md`.
- Does not read [Trends research](/docs/claude/showcase-trends-research)'s output. These two run independently of each other, only their parent merges what they produce.
- [Market research](/docs/claude/showcase-market-research), the last of the three, reads this skill's file back in, since sizing a market usually starts from who else is already in it.

</InOneMinute>
