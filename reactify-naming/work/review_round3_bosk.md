# Independent review, round 3: Bosk (fresh context, 2026-09-28)

VERDICT Bosk: FAIL - An earlier, live US application for BOSKI (Serial 99926179, Whitestone Labs, Inc., filed 7 July 2026, classes 9 and 42) covers AI agent, chatbot and NLP software and SaaS, and it is in use for a launched AI agent product at boski.com. BOSK is contained whole in BOSKI, and "bosky" is the English adjective formed from "bosk". My judgment is that this is a confusingly similar live mark in the company's own field (AI software).

Reviewer: independent subagent with fresh context. Every check below ran on 2026-09-28. Where a call is a judgment rather than a looked-up fact, it is labelled **JUDGMENT**.

## Language and radio test

Source: [Wiktionary, "bosk"](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/bosk), unless another link is given.

- **English:** "bosk" means "(archaic) a thicket; a small wood", from Middle English *bosk/busk* and Late Latin *boscus* ("wood, woodland"). The meaning is positive but obscure. On [Urban Dictionary](https://www.urbandictionary.com/define.php?term=bosk), the top entry is "good, great, enjoyable" (Northern Irish/Scots slang, 5,011 upvotes). There is also a minor "oral sex" entry (112 upvotes) and a personal-insult entry (1,520 upvotes). **JUDGMENT:** this is fringe slang and not disqualifying.
- **Serbian:** Wiktionary has no Serbo-Croatian entry. **JUDGMENT:** the word has no meaning in Serbian. The nearest forms are *bos* ("barefoot") and the first name *Boško*, both neutral.
- **German:** no entry, and **JUDGMENT:** no negative meaning. The only issue is its closeness to Bosch (see above).
- **Spanish, Italian, French:** no entry for "bosk" itself. It shares the Latin root *boscus* with *bosque*, *bosco* and *bosquet* (forest, grove), which are positive (**JUDGMENT** on how speakers will perceive it).
- **Hungarian:** no entry. Hungarian reads "s" as /ʃ/ ([Hungarian alphabet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hungarian_alphabet)), so "Bosk" is read as "boshk", which is even closer to Bosch. No negative meaning was found.
- **Other languages, for information:** Albanian "hornless goat", Maltese "wood, forest", West Frisian "forest; bundle; thicket". All harmless.
- **Radio test:** the pronunciation is /bɒsk/ in British English and /bɑsk/ in American English (Wiktionary), so there is one obvious way to say it, which passes. Spelling after hearing it is weaker. The pear variety **Bosc** is a homophone ([Bosc pear](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bosc_pear)), "Bosque" is another possible spelling, and the Boski founder's own post spells "Bosky". **JUDGMENT:** a marginal pass. People will sometimes need "B-O-S-K, k as in kite".

## Main residual risk

**The main risk is BOSKI (USPTO 99926179, Whitestone Labs, Inc., classes 9 and 42, filed 2026-07-07), together with the live Boski AI agent product.** It is the only item that meets the decision rule, and it does so on two counts:

- It is a live, similar mark in classes 9 and 42 in the US.
- It is a similar name used by a software company for a launched AI product.

The facts are verified: the filing, its status, the classes, the verbatim goods, the owner and the live product. Whether the marks are "confusingly similar" is my **JUDGMENT**, for the reasons given under Closest conflicts. The application is intent-to-use and has not been examined, so it could still be abandoned or narrowed. That uncertainty does not remove the risk now: its filing date comes before any filing Bosk could make, and the goods sit in exactly the space where the company builds and sells (AI software and agents).

Secondary risks, none of them disqualifying on its own:

- the proximity to Bosch, which already affects search results;
- the Bosc/Bosque spelling drift;
- the small identical-name Java library (bosk.works, boskworks);
- the bare .com, .io, .ai, .dev and .app, the GitHub name and the X handle are all unavailable, and bosklabs.com is taken.

If the founder still wants Bosk, the minimum step before committing is a US clearance opinion on BOSK against BOSKI 99926179. Until that is done, this review counts the name as failed.
