# Independent review, benchmark: Zanat (fresh context, 2026-09-29)

VERDICT Zanat: PASS - No live ZANAT mark in classes 9/35/42 at USPTO, EUIPO, WIPO or the Serbian office, and no established software or tech company or product with the name; the word means "craft/trade" in Serbian, and zanat.studio is available. The pass is a judgment call: two tiny same-name dev agencies (ZanatWorks, Zanat Digital Agency) and a well-known Bosnian furniture brand called Zanat make the namespace crowded, and every core TLD is taken.

## Language and radio test

- **Serbian, Croatian and Bosnian**: *zanat* means "trade, craft, skill; handicraft", a positive meaning that fits a product studio (https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/zanat). The Hungarian Wiktionary gives the same Serbo-Croatian meaning ("mesterség") and has no Hungarian-language entry (https://hu.wiktionary.org/wiki/zanat).
- **Albanian**: "craft, handwork, profession, art". One sense is "habit, skill or vice", but it is not negative in practice (https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/zanat).
- **Turkish** *zanaat* is "craft", and **Romanian** *artizanat* and **French** *artisanat* both mean "handicraft". The associations are positive.
- **English, German, French, Italian and Hungarian**: I found no dictionary word or vulgar homophone. The French Wiktionary has only a Kotava entry, "we serve" (https://fr.wiktionary.org/wiki/zanat).
- **Spanish**: *zanate* is a grackle, a common bird in Mexico and Central America (https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/zanate). This is a near-word, not the same word, and it is neutral.
- **Radio test (judgment): marginal pass.**
  - Serbs say ZAH-naht, with stress on the first syllable.
  - English speakers may say ZAN-ut or za-NAHT, so the stress is not fixed.
  - The spelling is phonetic, so after hearing it most people will write "Zanat". The likely misspellings are "Zannat", "Zanot" and "Zahnat".
  - It is short, has no silent letters and no ambiguous letter pairs.

## Main residual risk

The name is crowded, although nothing in it counts as a disqualifier.

- **Domains**: zanat.com was sold through Efty in 2025, and zanat.dev (March 2026) and zanat.app (June 2026, on Vercel) were registered this year. Someone may be launching a "Zanat" tech product right now, so the founder should expect no path to the .com and should re-check before filing.
- **Similar dev shops**: ZanatWorks (AI and software product studio, since October 2025) and the Kosovo agency Zanat Digital Agency, which is reachable at zanat.io, overlap in services. If either grows, clients of product and AI development work could confuse the names.
- **The furniture brand**: Zanat furniture owns search results for the bare word and holds a class 35 mark in Bosnia and Herzegovina, next to the Serbian market.
- **Unregistered class 9 marks**: the Turkish "zanat" class 9 mark (2025) and the USPTO ZANATECH class 9 application are outside the brief's scope or unrelated in goods. Neither blocks a filing, but both show that nobody holds a ZANAT software mark in the US, EU or Serbia yet. Filing early in classes 9 and 42 (and 35) would secure it.
